# 30-second demo video — script

Record the live app with **Play demo** (top bar). It runs the story by itself with captions; speed up or cut between steps in the editor. English only on screen and in the voice-over. Add subtitles.

| Time | On screen (Play demo step) | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s | Overview (Khonsu), Mars transit, day 47, "14 min one way" | "Day 47. Fourteen light-minutes from Earth. No doctor on board can be reached live." |
| 4–9 s | Status: **CAUTION**, heart rate 108, temperature 37.9 °C, guidance | "The morning check finds a fever and a fast pulse. The app tells the astronaut what to do right now." |
| 9–13 s | Trend watch + radiation budget | "It also catches slow changes before they cross a limit, and tracks radiation against NASA's career limit." |
| 13–16 s | 3D body with status markers | "The body shows which systems need attention." |
| 16–22 s | Report flies to Earth, clock ×600, reply comes back | "The report travels to Earth at the speed of light. The crew acts first; Earth follows." |
| 22–26 s | Crew board: CM-3 **ACT NOW** | "On board, the crew medical officer sees the whole crew at once." |
| 26–30 s | Night mode, sleep shift page, then app name | "And at night it switches to low-blue light, so checking your health does not cost you your sleep." |

## Tips
- Record at 1920×1080, browser zoom 110–125 %, hide bookmarks bar.
- Run **Reset link and settings** (Earth link page) before recording.
- Close the caption bar from the edit if you add your own subtitles.
- End card: app name (issue #1), team name, "NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026". No NASA logo.
- Say "self-assessment, does not diagnose" once, on screen or in the voice-over.
